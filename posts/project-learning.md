## Project learning: turning messy data into a clear story

Data can look chaotic at first, but with the right structure and questions it becomes much easier to understand. This is one of the reasons I enjoy working with data — it helps transform a confusing set of numbers into something meaningful.

In my projects, I focus on cleaning data, identifying patterns, and presenting the insights in a way that supports decisions. That process has taught me that communication matters just as much as technical skill.

A strong project is not just about code. It is about telling a clear story with evidence.
