## Why I build small projects that solve real problems

One of the biggest lessons I have learned while building projects is that even a small idea can become valuable when it solves a real pain point. I like to focus on projects that are useful, understandable, and easy to explain to other people.

Small projects teach more than theory because they force you to think through design, implementation, and trade-offs. They also help you explain your process clearly in interviews and portfolio conversations.

The goal is not to build everything at once. The goal is to learn by shipping something simple, useful, and thoughtfully designed.
